Autonomicor

Help test a new idea for small IT teams

Autonomicor is a proposed platform that finds, explains and fixes routine IT faults automatically, so a team of one to five people can run like a full operations team. This is an MVP that is testing whether it solves a real problem before anything is built. Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time - your honest views, criticism, will help most.

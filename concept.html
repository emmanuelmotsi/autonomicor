Autonomicor

Finds, explains and fixes routine IT faults automatically

Business concept statement

The product

Autonomicor is an online platform that finds, explains and fixes routine IT faults automatically, so a team of 1–5 can run like a full operations team. A lightweight collector installed on your network finds your servers, virtual machines, switches and routers and sets up monitoring automatically. When something breaks, Autonomicor explains in plain language what failed, why, and what it affects. For routine problems you have approved in advance, such as a stopped Windows service, it applies the fix, checks that the service has recovered, and records every action in an audit log.

A typical day with Autonomicor

At 10:14 a retail chain’s stock application stops responding. Within a minute Autonomicor detects the failure. It links it to the stopped application service on the server and posts a one-line explanation. Because “restart this service” is on the approved list, it restarts the service and confirms the application is back at 10:16. The IT administrator gets a summary instead of a call from the branch manager.
